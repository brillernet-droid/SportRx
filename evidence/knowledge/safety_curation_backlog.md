# Venue Entry Safety Curation Backlog

This backlog is a reviewer work queue, not an approved knowledge corpus and not
member-facing evidence. Its purpose is to complete the remaining 29 reviewed
cards needed before the internal Knowledge Lab can consider its 60-card
synthesis threshold.

| Queue | Target cards | What a card must change or bound |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Preparticipation screening and referral boundaries | 12 | External pathway scope, referral language, and non-diagnostic limitations. |
| Exercise-test safety and field-test stop rules | 8 | Benchmark stop conditions, supervision context, and protocol limits. |
| Chinese public guidance and local implementation | 5 | Chinese user-facing language or venue operating boundary only; never a score or norm. |
| Screening accuracy and implementation limitations | 4 | Why a screening route cannot become medical clearance, a risk percentage, or a prediction. |

## Review rules

1. Discover through the documented PubMed, OpenAlex, and Crossref workflow.
2. Deduplicate by DOI, PMID, and normalized title before review.
3. Retain only material that changes a product boundary, an external-pathway
   registry field, a test protocol, or a fixed member explanation.
4. A human reviewer must create the `KnowledgeCard`, state its limitations,
   select permitted question types, and approve it before indexing.
5. Do not upload copyrighted PDFs to GitHub. Public records contain metadata,
   stable links, and original concise summaries only.

Until these cards are reviewed, the current default Chinese provider remains
`research_required`; the Knowledge Lab cannot determine a member route.
