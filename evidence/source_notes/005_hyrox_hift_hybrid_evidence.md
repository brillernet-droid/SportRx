# Source Note 005: HYROX, HIFT, And Hybrid Competition Evidence

## Source Cluster

- Evidence IDs: `HYROX-PHYS-2025`, `HIFT-HYBRID-REVIEW`, `HIFT-DEFINITION`,
  `HIFT-FITNESS`, `CONCURRENT-TRAINING`, `INJ-CROSSFIT-SR`, `INJ-HIFT-SR`
- Topic: HYROX-style and high-intensity functional training evidence
- Evidence tier: C, with some B/C supporting literature
- Product area: HYROX Check, Training Profile, Starter Path

## What The Evidence Supports

The current evidence supports conservative framing:

- HYROX-style performance appears to involve both running and functional station
  work.
- Aerobic capacity, running ability, strength endurance, technical familiarity,
  and fatigue tolerance are plausible performance domains.
- HIFT and hybrid competitions are multi-domain rather than purely endurance or
  purely strength events.
- Concurrent endurance and strength training should be managed thoughtfully.

## What It Does Not Support

The evidence is not yet enough to support:

- validated HYROX readiness categories,
- finish-time prediction,
- injury-risk prediction,
- recreational first-timer percentiles,
- or SportRx-specific main-gap routing validation.
