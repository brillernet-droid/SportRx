# Claim Policy

SportRx should sound scientifically grounded without pretending to be validated.

## Allowed Claim Types

### Measurement Status

Allowed:

- "This area has not been tested yet."
- "At least two measured performance areas are needed before comparing a
  strongest area and a main gap."
- "This result describes your current measured training profile."

Avoid:

- "Your true weakness is..."
- "Your sport potential is..."
- "Your risk is low because your performance is high."

### Guideline-Based Activity Targets

Allowed:

- "General adult guidelines commonly recommend at least 150 minutes per week of
  moderate-intensity aerobic activity, plus muscle-strengthening activity on 2
  or more days per week."
- "This target is a public-health reference point, not a race-readiness score."

Avoid:

- "150 minutes per week means you are ready for HYROX."
- "Meeting the guideline means you have low injury risk."

### Safety Gate

Allowed:

- "SportRx detected information that should be clarified before automated
  training handoff."
- "Safety Gate is separate from performance scoring."

Avoid:

- "You are medically cleared."
- "You are safe to compete."
- "Your medical risk is X percent."

### HYROX / Hybrid Race

Allowed:

- "HYROX-style participation appears to require a combination of running,
  aerobic capacity, strength endurance, station familiarity, and work capacity."
- "SportRx currently treats this as an expert-informed training profile, not a
  validated prediction model."

Avoid:

- "You have an X percent chance of finishing HYROX."
- "Your HYROX readiness score is validated."
- "Your percentile is..."

### Starter Path

Allowed:

- "Based on the measured main gap, a reasonable starter focus is..."
- "Use this as a conservative 4-week starting block and retest."

Avoid:

- "This plan is proven to improve your HYROX result."
- "This plan prevents injury."

## Forbidden Claims Until Validated

- Completion probability.
- Injury-risk percentage.
- Fake percentiles or norms.
- Disease-specific exercise prescription.
- Medical clearance.
- Genetic or innate sport suitability.
- Validated SportRx readiness score.

## Tone Rule

Use measured, humble language:

- "suggests"
- "currently measured"
- "not tested"
- "reasonable next step"
- "needs validation"

Do not use overconfident language:

- "proves"
- "guarantees"
- "predicts"
- "diagnoses"
- "certifies"
