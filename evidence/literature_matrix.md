# Literature Matrix

This file tracks the evidence SportRx can cite or use internally. It is a
curated working matrix, not an exhaustive systematic review.

## Evidence Gaps To Fill

- Test-retest reliability of SportRx Hybrid Benchmark v1.
- Minimal detectable change for SportRx's station circuit.
- Whether two measured dimensions are enough for useful starter-path routing.
- Whether main-gap routing improves adherence or retest outcomes.
- Recreational HYROX first-timer data, not elite-only or race-result-only data.
- Chinese recreational athlete usability data.
- China-local physical activity guideline layer.
