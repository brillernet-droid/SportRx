base.

## Chinese UI Style

Use Chinese for navigation and explanation, but keep familiar sport-science and
event terms in English:

- HYROX
- Hybrid Race
- RPE
- Benchmark
- RowErg
- SkiErg
- Station circuit
- Training Profile

The product should feel like a Chinese performance-lab interface, not a literal
translation of English sport terms.
