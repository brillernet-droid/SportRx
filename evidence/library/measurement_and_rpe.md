# Measurement And RPE Library

## TEST-6MWT-ATS

- Title: ATS statement: guidelines for the six-minute walk test
- URL: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12091180/
- Evidence tier: B
- SportRx use:
  - protocol standardization example,
  - low-equipment aerobic testing discipline,
  - stopping/safety rule inspiration.
- Limit:
  - clinical functional-capacity test, not HYROX-specific.

## TEST-FIELD-ADULT

- Title: Criterion-Related Validity of Field-Based Fitness Tests in Adults
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8397016/
- Evidence tier: B
- SportRx use:
  - field-test validity framing,
  - need to document validity and reliability before claims.
- Limit:
  - does not validate SportRx's exact benchmark battery.

## TEST-FIELD-SAFETY

- Title: Feasibility and Safety of Field-Based Physical Fitness Tests
- URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40798-024-00799-1
- Evidence tier: B
- SportRx use:
  - reinforces caution around field-test implementation,
  - supports collecting feasibility and safety data in SportRx users.
- Limit:
  - adult field-test evidence remains limited when validity, reliability,
    feasibility, and safety are considered together.

## TEST-FIELD-RELIABILITY

- Title: Reliability of Field-Based Fitness Tests in Adults: A Systematic Review
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35064915/
- Evidence tier: B
- SportRx use:
  - supports test-retest reliability as a necessary benchmark property.
- Limit:
  - source access may be limited; do not overclaim without full review.

## MON-RPE-ACSM

- Title: What Is a Normal Rating of Perceived Exertion During Maximal Exercise Testing?
- Organization: ACSM
- URL: https://acsm.org/normal-rating-perceived-exertion-maximal-exercise/
- Evidence tier: B

## MON-SRPE-FOSTER

- Title: A new approach to monitoring exercise training
- Authors: Foster et al.
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11708692/
- Evidence tier: B
- SportRx use:
  - session-RPE as practical training-load monitoring concept.
- Limit:
  - should be interpreted with adherence, symptoms, and retest data.

## MON-SRPE-REVIEW

- Title: Session-RPE Method for Training Load Monitoring
- Authors: Haddad et al.
- URL: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnins.2017.00612/full
- Evidence tier: B
- SportRx use:
  - session-RPE validity/reliability context across sport settings.
- Limit:
  - subjective measure; not sufficient alone for performance or injury claims.
