# HYROX, HIFT, And Hybrid Competition Library

## HYROX-PHYS-2025

- Title: Acute physiological responses and performance determinants in Hyrox
- Journal: Frontiers in Physiology
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11994925/
- Evidence tier: C
- SportRx use:
  - emerging support for HYROX as a combined running and station-capacity event,
  - supports measuring aerobic/running and station-related domains.
- Limit:
  - early evidence; not enough for completion prediction or validated readiness score.

## HIFT-HYBRID-REVIEW

- Title: High Intensity Functional Training in Hybrid Competitions
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12550923/
- Evidence tier: C
- SportRx use:
  - hybrid competition domain framing,
  - supports multi-domain profile: endurance, strength endurance, work capacity.
- Limit:
  - review/scoping evidence, not SportRx validation.

## HIFT-DEFINITION

- Title: High-Intensity Functional Training definition and research implications
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6162410/
- Evidence tier: C
- SportRx use:
  - terminology and HIFT scope,
  - supports not reducing hybrid events to generic HIIT.
- Limit:
  - HIFT is broader than HYROX.
