# Safety Screening Library

## SAFE-EIM

- Title: Exercise is Medicine
- Organization: American College of Sports Medicine
- URL: https://acsm.org/EIM/
- Evidence tier: A
- SportRx use:
  - physical activity assessment concept,
  - referral/resource boundary,
  - non-diagnostic framing.
- Limit:
  - does not validate SportRx's Safety Gate implementation.

## SAFE-EIM-SCREEN

- Title: Exercise Preparticipation Health Screening Questionnaire for Exercise Professionals
- Organization: Exercise is Medicine
- URL: https://www.exerciseismedicine.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/EIM-exercise-preparticipation-screening.pdf
- Evidence tier: A
- SportRx use:
  - symptom and disease-history boundary concepts,
  - professional assessment routing.
- Limit:
  - do not reproduce proprietary questionnaire language wholesale.

## SAFE-PARQ

- Title: Physical Activity Readiness Questionnaire for Everyone / PAR-Q+
- Organization: ePARmed-X
- URL: https://eparmedx.com/par-q/
- Evidence tier: A
- SportRx use:
  - supports exercise readiness screening concept,
  - supports referral boundary when health concerns are present.
- Limit:
  - SportRx should link or route rather than copy the instrument.

## SAFE-ACSM-ALGO

- Title: Applying the ACSM Preparticipation Screening Algorithm to U.S. Adults
- URL: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7059860/
- Evidence tier: B
- SportRx use:
  - supports avoiding excessive barriers,
  - supports considering symptoms, known disease, activity level, and intended intensity.
- Limit:
  - not a SportRx validation study.
