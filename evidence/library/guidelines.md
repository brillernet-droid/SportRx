# Guidelines Library

## PA-WHO-2020

- Title: WHO guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour
- Organization: World Health Organization
- Year: 2020
- URL: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128
- NCBI Bookshelf: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK566046/
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33239350/
- Evidence tier: A
- SportRx use:
  - adult aerobic activity reference range,
  - muscle-strengthening reference,
  - "some activity is better than none" progression language.
- Limit:
  - public-health recommendation, not HYROX readiness or performance prediction.

## PA-CDC-ADULT

- Title: Adult Activity: An Overview
- Organization: CDC
- URL: https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html
- Evidence tier: A
- SportRx use:
  - simple user-facing explanation of weekly adult activity target,
  - reinforcement of 150 minutes/week and 2 strengthening days/week.
- Limit:
  - U.S. public-health summary, not individualized sport prescription.
